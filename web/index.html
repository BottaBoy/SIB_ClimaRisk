Methodology & Data Sources

Thesis-oriented summary of the modeling chain and current MVP backend/runtime status.

Hazard

  • STORM and STORM_CMCC tropical cyclone hazards (Nadia Bloemendaal datasets).
  • Wind maps use the full 10,000-year NA catalogs currently available on the server.
  • Probabilistic cyclone risk framing for water infrastructure exposure.
  • Frequency normalization is handled on a copy in the backend architecture (idempotent design).

Exposure

  • Reference page uses complete Guadeloupe water+electric infrastructure datasets.
  • Exposure means the set of assets at stake (infrastructures, habitations, etc.).
  • User uploads: CSV/XLSX and GIS formats (GeoJSON/GPKG) are accepted by the API contract.
  • Lines and polygons are disaggregated to points before impact calculation, then reaggregated.

Impact & Dependency

  • MVP production target is the Eberenz 2021 tropical cyclone impact function.
  • Fallback engine uses 4 states (S0/S1/S2/S3), component health, and electricity-to-water propagation.
  • All water assets are conservatively treated as electricity-dependent in this iteration.
